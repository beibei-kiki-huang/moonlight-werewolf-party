# Moonlight — Play Beyond Borders

A bilingual Werewolf game, without a moderator. Interface/narration: English/Chinese. Quick Chat: English, Chinese, Korean, Japanese and Spanish. Typed chat stays as written.

## Solo review

https://moonlight-werewolf-party.onrender.com/demo.html?lang=en

The clearly disclosed 60-second scripted tour creates no room and uses no real opponents, credentials or multiplayer state. Previous/Next pause and preserve choices; Pause/Resume, Replay and Exit are available. Optional fictional Seer/Werewolf/Witch examples are separate. Real night stages each last 60 seconds; the tour is condensed. Voting explains positive plurality, tied candidate speaking, one runoff and last words. It never submits a real ballot. Chinese tour: lang=zh. The main story demonstrates EN/ZH; live presets additionally support KO/JA/ES.

## Play with friends

https://moonlight-werewolf-party.onrender.com/

Classic needs 5–8 players; Duel needs 2. Create a room with a nickname, share the invitation/code, and have everyone Ready. The lobby names exact blockers. No installation or accounts. Host starts/rematches; Moonlight moderates phases and results.

Classic defaults to seat-order discussion (45 seconds each; host choices 30/45/60), or choose Open Discussion before starting. Only current speakers can send text/presets in structured/tie/last-word turns. Anyone eligible can choose then explicitly confirm a secret ballot. Abstentions do not compete with candidates. An initial tie gives each tied candidate 60 seconds, then other living players vote once. A second tie/all abstain/no eligible runoff voters means no elimination. Daytime eliminations get 30 seconds of last words; night deaths get none. The unique positive leader can be eliminated even with one vote. Duel remains opponent-versus-center, with no abstention/runoff/night kill.

Each Classic night keeps the same full Werewolf/Seer/Witch 60-second schedule regardless of role availability. Private actions stay owner-only, with accurate faction findings and one-use potion inventory. Wolf target ties/fallback use server randomness. Dawn publishes actual eliminated players or 平安夜, never potion usage or cause. Original role paintings remain; mobile uses compact phase timing, role access, conversation, voting and results.

## Communication across languages

Choose Chat language inside public or authorized Pack chat. The same ten phrase IDs display in your selection; the language control on a phrase expands equivalents. This does not change the English/Chinese interface/narrator. Typed messages remain original. Colors and readable names identify participants. No external translation call.

Seven/eight-player Classic begins with two wolves. Only living teammates can use Pack Chat during the current 60-second Werewolf phase; it closes and clears at exit. Outsiders/dead wolves receive no messages or metadata. Presets support all five languages. The solo pack conversation is explicitly fictional.

## Honest limits and credits

Free-host wake-up can take about a minute; restart/redeploy ends rooms. Refresh recovers existing same-browser seats; keep the host available for rematches. No host migration, restart recovery, public matchmaking, accounts, permanent messaging or live voice chat. Final physical-device/human group validation remains a creator checklist, not an automated-testing claim.
