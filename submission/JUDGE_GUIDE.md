# Moonlight — Play Beyond Borders

A bilingual Werewolf game, without a moderator.

## Review solo

Open https://moonlight-werewolf-party.onrender.com/demo.html?lang=en

The Guided Demo is a clearly labeled fictional, condensed 60-second tour. No real opponents are present and no room is created. Try role reveal, phase guidance, recipient-language Quick Chat, draft/confirmed voting and results. Previous, Next, Pause/Resume, Exit and Replay are available. Previous/Next pause playback and preserve selections. Replay resets everything. The optional Seer, Werewolf and Witch interactions are clearly separate fictional nights outside the default 60-second storyline. Seer examples include both factions, confirmation and hidden/reopenable clues. Werewolf examples show teammates, agreement, tied attacks and a labeled scripted pack conversation. Sound is not required. Chinese tour: change EN / 中, or open the same URL with lang=zh. The sample tour does not prove multiplayer; the live game is separate.

## Play for real

Open https://moonlight-werewolf-party.onrender.com/

Choose Moonlight Duel for two people, or Classic for five to eight. Create a private room with a nickname, share the invitation/code, and let everyone choose Ready. No accounts or installation. Each player chooses English or Simplified Chinese independently.

The server keeps roles/actions private, guides phases and determines results. Quick Chat uses ten fixed bilingual phrases; each recipient sees their selected language. Optional equivalents can be expanded. Ordinary typed chat is not translated. The host starts/rematches, but nobody needs to sit out as moderator.

## Wolf Pack Chat in real Classic

Seven/eight-player Classic starts with two wolves. While at least two remain alive, their private chat opens only during the fixed 25-second Werewolf action phase. It appears below attack controls. Typed messages stay as written; presets appear in each recipient's selected language. Messages vanish at phase closure and are never part of public room history. Five/six-player Classic and Duel do not have a teammate chat. The solo demo uses fictional participants, not a real room.

## Honest limitations

Render Free may take about one minute to wake. Restart/redeploy ends in-memory rooms; same-browser refresh recovery does not survive a server restart. No automatic host migration, public matchmaking, accounts, permanent messages, live voice chat or automated free-text translation.

The creator has reported real two-person Duel playtests. Independent Socket.IO acceptance tests cover Classic and Duel. These are not a substitute for a documented mixed-language five-to-six-person human session with a Werewolf beginner.
